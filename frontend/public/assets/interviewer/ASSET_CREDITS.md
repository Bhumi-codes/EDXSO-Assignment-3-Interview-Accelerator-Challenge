# Interviewer assets

professional-interviewer.glb is a modified Microsoft Rocketbox Business_Male_03 avatar, including suit, hair and facial rig. Its face texture includes a light-beard modification. The office background was generated for this project. No candidate photographs or camera recordings are included.

Sources:
- https://github.com/microsoft/Microsoft-Rocketbox/tree/master/Assets/Avatars/Professions/Business_Male_03
- Conversion tool: https://github.com/927tanmay/react-ai-voice-avatar/blob/main/scripts/convert-rocketbox.py
